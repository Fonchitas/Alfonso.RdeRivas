Alfonso is a multimedia designer trained at UDIT Madrid who works across visual identity, 3D production, music composition and web development.

He's interested in the point where these disciplines intersect: projects where image, sound and interaction tell the same story. His work has been recognised with the ANFACO Design Award and a selection at the Laus Inbook 2026.

Tools: Cinema 4D, Houdini, After Effects, Logic Pro X, HTML/CSS/JS and Three.js.
Typefaces used on this site: Diatype by Dinamo, Herbik by Daniel Veneklaas.
