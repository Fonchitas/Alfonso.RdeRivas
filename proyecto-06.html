Percepta Wines

Percepta Wines is a label concept that treats every quality wine drinkers usually overlook (noble rot, petrol notes, tartrate crystals) as a compound detected and diagnosed on the bottle itself, like a lab report.

Each label reads like a warning, not a decoration: the same instinct that makes people distrust these traits turned into the whole point of the design.
