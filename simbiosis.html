Simbiosis

Simbiosis is a cultural mediation system for emerging painters. The work doesn't end on the canvas: each collaboration translates into visual identity, original ambient music and generative 3D visuals, brought together on a web platform with an interactive Lab. The project is built on two real collaborations: Col.01 OLAS with Paz Alonso González, and Col.02 CAMPO with Rosa Naranjo.
