Music Production

Besides design, I compose the music for some of my own projects. I work in Logic Pro X, mainly on ambient recordings and original compositions meant to accompany visual pieces — the soundtrack for Simbiosis comes from this same process.

Each track usually starts from a texture or a simple rhythmic pattern that I build layer by layer, closer to constructing an atmosphere than following a conventional musical structure. I'm especially interested in where sound and design meet: how a harmonic progression can reinforce a piece's visual rhythm, or how silence and space work the same way in both languages. Most of these compositions are born for a specific project, but several have ended up taking on a life of their own beyond it.
